Troubleshooting / FAQs¶

  • Upload not visible? Check the Globus transfer status, and check whether you are looking at the personal or the group context.
  • Black image? Adjust brightness, or check that conversion finished.
  • Annotation tools greyed out? The view is not locked. See Interactive Annotation.
  • Where do I annotate? In the browser, on the scene. Open Interactive Annotation from the sidebar. The sidebar's Virtual Desktop and the ROI dialog's Annotate are not part of this workflow; Download in that dialog gives you a local copy of a region.

If problems persist, contact the site administrator.

About¶

This portal supports:

  • Large-scale multimodal microscopy data
  • Cloud-enabled storage and streaming
  • Neuroglancer-based visualization
  • Interactive and AI-assisted annotation, including nnInteractive
