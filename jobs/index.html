This tutorial covers the Jobs page, where background work is tracked.

Overview¶

Work that takes longer than a request runs as a background job: SISF conversion, flatfield fitting, tile stitching, spot detection, and microCT segmentation. Open Jobs in the sidebar to see them.

Each row shows the job type, its status, and progress. You can leave the page or close the browser while a job runs.

Actions¶

The Actions menu shows what the job's current status allows:

  • View Details shows the parameters the job was submitted with, and the error message if it failed.
  • Start Job submits a job that was created but never sent to a worker.
  • Cancel Job stops a job that is pending, running, or paused.
  • Restart Job submits it again. It appears once the job has stopped, whether it was paused, canceled, or failed.
  • Delete Job removes the row.

Troubleshooting¶

  • A job stays at 0%: it is queued and waiting for a free worker.
  • A job failed: open View Details and read the error. For conversion, the usual causes are an image size or channel count that does not match the file.
